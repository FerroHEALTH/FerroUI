- A component's form is an argument of its one function or a field of what it draws, and the drawn HTML is unchanged. `table::view::DataTable` carries its `shape` (a `Shape`) and is built with `DataTable::new(caption, columns, body)`, `.with_sorting` and `.with_shape`; `KeyedCombobox` carries its `remote` search. `title_cell(level, href, name, sub)` takes an `Option<u32>` level, `view::dialog::confirm_dialog(locale, confirmation, stakes, form, confirm)` its `Stakes` and an optional `ConfirmForm`, `info_dialog(locale, explanation, body, width)` a `Width`, `view::compare::compare(locale, table, presentation)` a `Presentation`, `view::side_panel::side_panel(panel, shortcut)`, `view::shortcuts::shortcut_legend(locale, groups, go_to)` and `view::secret::secret_state(locale, state, id)` an `Option`, `calendar::Moment::parse(text, with_time, order)` a `DateOrder` and `table::model::Paging::write(query, sizes)` the console's `Sizes`. `docs/consoles.md` lists each call.

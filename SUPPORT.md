# Getting help

## I have a question

Start at the [README](README.md) and the crate documentation on docs.rs
(<https://docs.rs/ferrohealth-ui>). The gallery screenshots under
`docs/screenshots/` show every component in every theme. If those do not
answer it, open an issue through the
[issue chooser](https://github.com/FerroHEALTH/FerroUI/issues/new/choose).

There is no commercial support offering and no service-level agreement. A
commercial licence or any other business question goes to Cadasto B.V., the
Licensor: [info@cadasto.com](mailto:info@cadasto.com) or
<https://www.cadasto.com/contact/>.

## I found a defect

[Open an issue](https://github.com/FerroHEALTH/FerroUI/issues/new/choose)
when a component is wrong, missing, fails WCAG 2.2 AA in a theme, or breaks a
keyboard path. A screenshot from the gallery and the brand and theme it shows
help.

## I found a vulnerability

Do not open an issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
